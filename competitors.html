Three Firms Competing For The Same Garden Grove Homeowner

Prepared for Jeong Kim · 2 October 2026 · AG1 Consulting
First, Something We Noticed About Your Own Listing
Your address and your service area do not match

Your website gives an address in Garden Grove, CA 92841 — Orange County. It then says you serve “Los Angeles, CA, and the surrounding areas”, and the phone number on it is a 213 number, which is central Los Angeles.

Garden Grove to Los Angeles is roughly thirty-five miles. Those are two different markets with different homeowners, different competitors, and different ADU rules set by different city councils.

⚠️ We are not saying this is wrong — plenty of contractors genuinely travel. But it does mean we could not tell which market you actually want to win, so we looked at the one you are physically sitting in. If the answer is really Los Angeles, tell us on the call and this page changes.

The Three, And Why We Picked Them

Each one makes a different argument. We chose them for that, not because they are the biggest.

1. Go ADU — goadu.com · Burbank · the specialist

Started in 2015 doing renovations and general construction. In 2020 they dropped all of it and now build nothing but ADUs. Co-led by Gil Vaisman and Hugo Ruiz.

Their website runs to 270 pages. Yours runs to six. They have a blog, a financing page, and a measured website — Google Analytics, Tag Manager and a heatmap tool that records how visitors move around the page.

⭐ The argument they are making is focus. A homeowner comparing a company that does only ADUs against a company that does ADUs plus eight other things will assume the specialist is better at it, whether or not that is true.

2. Avorino — avorino.com · Orange County · CSLB #1107538 · the advertiser

Seven-plus years, design-build, publishes its licence number. 247 pages. Blog, financing, free estimates, reviews.

🔴 They carry a Google Ads conversion tag. That is not a general analytics tool — it exists for one purpose, which is to measure which advert produced which enquiry. It means Avorino is spending money on advertising right now and tracking what comes back from it.

And they have a page at avorino.com/garden-grove-adu titled “Garden Grove ADU Regulations & Builders”. It is 4,097 words long and says “Garden Grove” ninety-three times.

⭐ Note what it is about: the rules, not the sales pitch. It is written for a homeowner who is still working out whether an ADU is even allowed on their lot — months before they are ready to hire anyone. By the time that person wants quotes, Avorino has already been helpful once.

3. Horizon Remodeling — horizonremodelinginc.com · CSLB #938230 · the one your size

Fifteen-plus years, publishes its licence number, serves Garden Grove. Their whole site is seven pages — almost exactly the size of yours.

🔴 And it carries Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and a Meta pixel. A seven-page site, fully instrumented.

They have a page at horizonremodelinginc.com/adu-contractor-in-garden-grove/, headed “#1 ADU Contractor in Garden Grove”. It is 3,537 words on that one page.

⭐ This is the one that matters most, and here is why. Horizon is not a big company with a big marketing department. They are roughly your size. So “I am too small for this” is not what is separating you from them.

The Two Findings That Should Actually Bother You
🔴 1. Two companies have a single page about Garden Grove bigger than your entire website

Avorino's Garden Grove page is 4,097 words. Horizon's is 3,537 words. Your whole site, every page, all nine trades, is 1,459 words.

So a Garden Grove homeowner typing “ADU Garden Grove” into Google finds a Burbank company and an Orange County company who have each written thousands of words about their own town — and the contractor who actually lives there has written almost nothing.

⭐ You have the one thing neither of them can buy, which is actually being local. It is simply not written down anywhere.

🔴 2. Your financing is not the advantage we thought it was

On the audit page we flagged customer financing as the strongest signal on your site. We were half wrong, and you should hear the correction from us rather than find it out later.

All three of these competitors offer financing too. So it is not a differentiator in this market — it is the entry fee.

⭐ It still tells us your job values are high, which was the useful part. But it will not win you a job against any of these three, because they can all say the same sentence.

What We Are Deliberately Not Claiming
⛔ Because the honest limits matter more than the findings
  • We do not know what any of these three earn, how many jobs they complete, or whether they are profitable. Page counts and tracking tags are not revenue.
  • A bigger website is not proof of a better business. It is proof of investment in being found. Those are different things and the second does not guarantee the first.
  • We have not verified anyone's licence status, including yours. We read numbers off websites.
  • We did not assess their build quality, their reviews in detail, or their pricing. You will know more about all three than we do.
  • ⚠️ A fourth firm appeared in search results with a Garden Grove page that did not load when we checked it, so we left it out rather than describe a page we could not read.
  • Years in business, licence numbers and locations are quoted from each company's own website and from public directory listings. We have not independently confirmed them.
So What Does This Change?
  • 1. Pick a market. Garden Grove or Los Angeles. You cannot out-write both, and right now the site points at one while sitting in the other.
  • 2. Pick the trade you want more of. Go ADU dropped eight services to win one. You do not have to go that far, but nine equal trades is nine half-arguments.
  • 3. Get measurement on before anything else. ⭐ Every competitor can see what their website does. Until you can, any money you spend is unmeasurable — including money you would spend with us.
  • 4. Write the page your competitors wrote about your own town. They are describing Garden Grove from thirty miles away. You are in it.
  • 5. Stop leading with financing. Keep it, but it is table stakes here, not a differentiator.
Three is the cheapest and the first. ⭐ You asked for one more job a month. None of the rest of this can be judged until something is counting.

A Burbank company and an Orange County company have each written more about Garden Grove than you have.

You are the one who lives there.
